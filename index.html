The Forest That Forgot to Sing

Beyond the last old road lies Veyra, where rivers remember every footstep and the trees once answered the moon. Now the song is thinning. Gather your guardians, mend what can be mended, and discover why the Rust Horde is crossing the fern sea.

One shared goal

Fill the Circular Loop while all four forest regions stay alive. Everyone takes two actions on their turn. No one wins alone.

How to play

Always available
1 · Take your turnPlayers act in lobby order. Spend 2 action points, then pass to the next guardian.
2 · Choose togetherGather fallen wood. Plant to heal a region. Harvest gives more wood but harms that region.
3 · Close the LoopRecycle scrap, Sing, and win d20 fights to build the Loop. Recycling makes tools without new extraction.
4 · Watch the mapEach round the Rust Horde strains every region. If any region reaches zero, the team loses.
5 · Win or loseWin immediately at 100 Loop while every region is alive. Lose if a region reaches zero, or round 10 ends before the Loop is full.
6 · Share your giftsEach guardian has one once-per-round ability that helps another player. Point at a region for action details and consequences.

How to play

On your turn: choose 2 actions, then pass. Tap an action, then a region. You cannot act out of turn.
Action guide: Gather +2 fallen wood. Plant costs 2 wood and heals 20. Harvest gains 4 wood but harms the region by 16. Recycle spends 1 scrap for a tool and +9 Loop. Sing heals 8 and adds +9 Loop. Fight rolls a d20; 11+ wins.
Round end: after everyone passes, every region loses 4 health. Win immediately at 100 Loop with all regions alive. Lose if any region reaches 0 or round 10 ends first.
Help a teammate: use your role gift once each round.
